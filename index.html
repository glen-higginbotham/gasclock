Economic and Policy Context

Australia is one of the world's largest exporters of liquefied natural gas (LNG). We are shipping tens of billions of dollars worth of gas overseas annually.

Multinational energy operators export non-renewable Australian resources while reporting minimal taxable super-profits and the Australian people are being screwed while private, faceless corporations are laughing all the way to the bank.

What This Means for Everyday Australians

Instead of taxing the super-profits of multinational gas corporations to help cool the economy and fund services, the government leaves the burden of fighting inflation to the Reserve Bank. That means everyday mortgage holders and renters get crushed by interest rate hikes, while resource giants pay minimal tax.

How Do Other Countries Do It?

Norway taxes offshore petroleum profits at an effective rate of 78%. Instead of giving away their natural resources for virtually nothing, they saved that money into a national sovereign wealth fund now worth over US$1.7 trillion. Australia has shipped away comparable amounts of gas while everyday Australians are left holding the bill.
