Why This Revenue Matters to Everyday Australians

Australia is one of the world's leading exporters of liquefied natural gas (LNG), yet the existing Petroleum Resource Rent Tax (PRRT) regime is notoriously full of deductions, uplift rates, and carry-forward credits. The Australian government consistently earns more revenue from the tax on beer than from offshore gas exports.

Taking Pressure Off Interest Rates

When governments lack corporate resource revenues, inflation is disproportionately fought through Reserve Bank cash rate increases. Every rate hike punishes mortgage holders and renters instead of super-profitable offshore corporations.

Funding Vital Cost-of-Living Relief

A 25% revenue tax would raise roughly A$18.5 billion annually. That is enough to universalise bulk-billing with Medicare, deliver significant childcare subsidies, and wipe student HECS indexation.

Protecting Our Sovereign Resources

Countries like Norway capture up to 78% of oil and gas earnings for their sovereign wealth fund. Senator David Pocock’s proposed 25% levy ensures that Australia's non-renewable national wealth directly benefits Australians before it is exported forever.
